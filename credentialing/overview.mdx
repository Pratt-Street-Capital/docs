## What Credentialing Covers

Credentialing is not a single event — it is an ongoing process that spans the entire life of a provider's relationship with each payer. Walker Health Services manages all of the following on your behalf:

- **Initial enrollment** — submitting applications to establish your providers as in-network with target payers for the first time
- **CAQH profile management** — creating, updating, and re-attesting your providers' Council for Affordable Quality Healthcare profiles, which most commercial payers draw from when processing applications
- **Network maintenance** — monitoring each payer relationship to ensure participation agreements remain active and in good standing
- **Re-credentialing** — completing the periodic renewal applications that payers require every two to three years to keep your providers in-network

## Why Credentialing Matters

Billing for services before a provider is credentialed — or after their credentials have lapsed — carries serious financial risk. Claims submitted out-of-network are routinely denied or reimbursed at significantly reduced rates, and some payers will retroactively recoup payments if a credentialing gap is later discovered. For behavioral health and residential treatment providers operating on tight margins, even a short lapse in credentialing can result in thousands of dollars in lost or clawed-back revenue.

In-network status also affects your clients. Many patients specifically seek out in-network providers to minimize their out-of-pocket costs, and being listed in a payer's directory can meaningfully increase referral volume.

<Note>
  Credentialing can take 90–180 days from application submission to approval. Starting the enrollment process well before a provider begins seeing patients is essential to avoiding billing gaps.
</Note>
