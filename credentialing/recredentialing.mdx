Getting your providers credentialed is only the beginning. Most insurance payers require re-credentialing every two to three years to verify that your providers' licenses, insurance, and professional standing are still current. Missing a re-credentialing deadline can result in loss of in-network status — and the billing disruptions and revenue losses that follow. Walker Health Services proactively tracks every re-credentialing cycle across your entire provider roster so no deadline is ever missed.

## What Triggers Re-Credentialing

Re-credentialing can be triggered by several different circumstances, not just the standard renewal cycle. Our team monitors all of the following on your behalf:

- **Expiration cycles** — the standard payer-required renewal, typically every 24–36 months from the date of original credentialing
- **Change of address** — moving your practice location or adding a new site often requires payer notification and may trigger a credentialing review
- **New or updated licenses** — adding a new state license, specialty certification, or DEA registration may need to be reported to payers
- **Payer-initiated audits** — payers occasionally conduct mid-cycle reviews of provider credentials, particularly for behavioral health and substance use disorder providers

<Info>
  Some payers have rolling re-credentialing cycles tied to the original enrollment date, while others align all providers to a fixed calendar cycle. Tracking these differences manually is difficult — our team maintains a dedicated schedule for every payer relationship in your organization.
</Info>

## Our Re-Credentialing Process

Walker Health Services manages the full re-credentialing lifecycle with a structured five-step process designed to ensure continuity of your in-network status at every renewal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Track Re-Credentialing Due Dates">
    We maintain a comprehensive calendar of every provider's re-credentialing deadline across all enrolled payers. Our system flags upcoming renewals well in advance so the process begins with enough lead time to complete before the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify You 90 Days in Advance">
    We contact your team 90 days before each re-credentialing deadline with a summary of what is needed, which payers are involved, and what the timeline looks like. This gives you enough time to gather updated documents without rushing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather Updated Documentation">
    We work with you to collect any documentation that has changed since the original credentialing — updated licenses, renewed malpractice certificates, new attestations — and refresh the provider's CAQH profile with current information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Re-Credentialing Application">
    We complete and submit the re-credentialing application to each payer according to that payer's specific process, whether through CAQH, an online portal, or a paper form. We track confirmation of receipt for every submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Up and Confirm Continued Network Participation">
    We actively follow up with payers on pending re-credentialing applications and confirm in writing that your provider's network participation has been renewed before the deadline passes. We file all confirmation letters and updated effective dates in your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## CAQH Maintenance and Re-Credentialing

Keeping your providers' CAQH profiles current is one of the most impactful things you can do to speed up re-credentialing across multiple payers. Most commercial payers pull directly from CAQH when processing renewals, which means an outdated or expired profile creates delays across every payer simultaneously.

Walker Health Services monitors CAQH attestation dates for all of your providers and re-attests profiles proactively — before the 120-day expiration — to ensure your profiles are always in an active, payer-ready state. When re-credentialing applications are due, an up-to-date CAQH profile means payers can process your renewals faster with fewer requests for additional information.

<Warning>
  Failing to re-credential on time can result in termination from payer networks and claim denials retroactive to the lapse date. In some cases, payers may recoup payments made during a gap period even if services were legitimately rendered. Proactive management of re-credentialing deadlines is not optional — it is a core protection for your revenue.
</Warning>

## Common Re-Credentialing Document Requirements

While requirements vary by payer, the documents most commonly requested during re-credentialing include:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Current State Licenses" icon="id-card">
    Renewed, active licensure for every state in which the provider practices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Malpractice Insurance Certificate" icon="file-medical">
    Current certificate of professional liability coverage showing your organization as the insured.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DEA Certificate" icon="circle-check">
    Renewed DEA registration, if applicable to the provider's scope of practice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CAQH Attestation" icon="user-doctor">
    An active, recently attested CAQH profile with current practice and provider information.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Board Certifications" icon="shield-halved">
    Updated copies of any specialty board certifications, if required by the payer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="W-9" icon="file-medical">
    A current IRS W-9 for your organization, particularly if your tax information has changed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How We Notify You of Upcoming Deadlines

You will never be caught off guard by a re-credentialing deadline. Our team uses a multi-touch notification approach to keep you informed well ahead of every renewal:

- **90-day notice** — an initial outreach to your designated contact with a full list of upcoming renewals and documentation needs
- **60-day check-in** — a follow-up confirming which documents have been received and flagging anything still outstanding
- **30-day alert** — a final reminder if any applications have not yet been submitted or if payer responses are still pending
- **Completion confirmation** — a written summary once re-credentialing is confirmed, including the renewed effective date and updated network participation details

<Tip>
  Designate a single point of contact at your organization for credentialing communications. This ensures our outreach reaches the right person quickly and prevents delays caused by misdirected messages or staff turnover.
</Tip>
