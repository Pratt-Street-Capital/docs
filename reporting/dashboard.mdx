Your dashboard is a live window into your revenue cycle health. At a glance, you can see how much has been billed, what has been collected, where claims stand, and where money may be sitting uncollected. Walker Health Services designed this view to give you the clarity you need to make informed decisions about your practice's financial performance — without having to dig through spreadsheets or wait for end-of-month reports.

## Key Metrics Displayed

Your dashboard surfaces the most critical revenue cycle indicators in real time. These metrics update as claims are processed, payments are posted, and denials are worked.

## Dashboard Sections Overview

Your dashboard is organized into four core sections, each designed to surface a specific dimension of your revenue cycle performance.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Claims Summary" icon="list-check">
    A snapshot of all active claims broken down by status — submitted, pending, paid, and denied. Use this section to monitor claim volume and identify bottlenecks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment Activity" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    A timeline of payments posted to your account, including insurance remittances and patient payments. Helps you track cash flow day by day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Denial Tracking" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A running log of denied claims with denial reason codes and current appeal status. Your billing team works these denials on your behalf and updates the log as they are resolved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AR Aging" icon="hourglass-half">
    A breakdown of outstanding balances segmented by how long they have been in accounts receivable. This section helps you understand where aged debt is concentrated.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Access Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is accessible through the Walker Health Services client portal. If you have not yet received your login credentials or need to reset access, reach out to your dedicated account manager directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Your Account Manager">
    Email or call your account manager to request portal credentials. New clients typically receive access within two business days of onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In to the Client Portal">
    Use the credentials provided to sign in at the Walker Health Services portal. You will be prompted to set a secure password on your first login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Practice">
    If your organization has multiple locations or provider groups, select the appropriate practice from the dropdown to filter your dashboard view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Date Range">
    Use the date range selector at the top of the dashboard to filter metrics by a specific billing period, month, or custom range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review your dashboard weekly to catch trends early and address issues before they impact cash flow.
</Tip>

## How to Read the AR Aging Report

The AR Aging section breaks your outstanding receivables into time-based buckets so you can quickly identify which balances require the most urgent attention. The older a balance, the harder it typically is to collect — which is why your billing team prioritizes aging claims proactively.

| Bucket | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **0–30 Days** | Recently submitted claims still within normal adjudication timelines. These are healthy and expected. |
| **31–60 Days** | Claims approaching the follow-up threshold. Your billing team monitors these for payer response. |
| **61–90 Days** | Claims that have not yet been paid or denied and require active follow-up. Your billing team escalates these for priority outreach to the payer. |
| **90+ Days** | Aged balances that may be at risk. These receive priority attention from your billing team and may involve appeals, resubmissions, or escalation. |

A well-managed AR aging report should have the majority of your outstanding balance in the 0–30 day bucket. If you notice a growing percentage in the 61–90 or 90+ ranges, flag this with your account manager immediately.

<Note>
  Your Walker Health account manager will walk through your AR aging report with you on your monthly performance review call. These calls are scheduled automatically and are an opportunity to ask questions, review trends, and align on billing priorities for the coming month.
</Note>
