Regular reporting keeps you informed on every dollar billed on your behalf. Walker Health Services produces a structured set of reports that give you full visibility into your claims pipeline — from the moment a charge is entered through final payment or denial resolution. Whether you need a high-level summary for leadership or a detailed breakdown for your operations team, your reports are designed to deliver the right information at the right time.

## Available Report Types

Each report type covers a distinct area of your revenue cycle. Together, they give you a comprehensive picture of billing activity, payer behavior, and collection performance.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Claims Status Report" icon="file-medical">
    A claim-level view of every submission processed during the reporting period. Each claim is labeled with its current status — open, pending, paid, or denied — along with the payer, date of service, billed amount, and any applied adjustments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Denial Summary Report" icon="ban">
    A summary of all denied claims organized by denial reason code, payer, and rendering provider. This report helps you and your billing team identify systemic issues that may be driving avoidable revenue loss.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payer Performance Report" icon="building-columns">
    A payer-by-payer analysis of average reimbursement rates and adjudication timelines. Use this report to understand which payers pay well, which pay slowly, and where contractual rates may need renegotiation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Charge Entry Report" icon="pen-to-square">
    A log of all charges posted to your account, organized by date of service. This report confirms that all clinical encounters have been captured and submitted for billing in a timely manner.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collection Report" icon="scale-balanced">
    A side-by-side comparison of charges billed versus payments received for a given period. This is your primary tool for tracking net revenue performance and identifying collection gaps.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Report Delivery

Walker Health Services delivers your reports through multiple channels depending on your preference and the type of report. You can choose one delivery method or combine them to meet the needs of different stakeholders in your organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Client Portal">
    All standard reports are available on demand through your Walker Health client portal. You can log in at any time, select a date range, and download reports in PDF or CSV format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scheduled Email Delivery">
    You can opt in to receive reports automatically on a recurring schedule — weekly, monthly, or both. Reports are sent as PDF attachments to your designated billing contact or administrative team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monthly Performance Review">
    Key reports are reviewed in detail during your monthly call with your account manager. Your manager will walk through highlights, flag any concerns, and answer questions in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Request a Custom Report

Standard reports cover the most common billing and financial tracking needs, but your practice may have unique reporting requirements. Custom reports can be built to match your internal formats, filter by specific payers or providers, or surface metrics not included in the default set.

<Info>
  To request a custom report, contact your account manager with a description of what you need. Include the data points, date range, grouping preferences, and preferred delivery format. Most custom reports are delivered within three to five business days.
</Info>

## Report Frequency

You control how often you receive your reports. Walker Health supports three delivery frequencies, which can be configured independently for different report types.

| Frequency | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| **Weekly** | Claims status, charge entry, and payment activity — ideal for practices with high claim volume or active denial management needs. |
| **Monthly** | Payer performance, collection summaries, and denial trend analysis — best reviewed alongside your monthly performance call. |
| **On-Demand** | Any report, any time. Log in to the portal or contact your account manager to pull a report for a specific period or event. |

<Tip>
  Use your denial summary reports to identify patterns across payers and reason codes. When the same denial appears repeatedly, it often signals a documentation gap, credentialing issue, or authorization problem that — once resolved — can unlock a meaningful amount of withheld revenue.
</Tip>
