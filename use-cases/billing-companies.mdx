Trekton gives billing companies a market-level view of negotiated rates so you can benchmark your book of business, identify underpayments faster, and walk into payer negotiations with data. This page walks through a typical workflow from pulling rate data to exporting a report you can share with clients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull rate data for your codes and geography">
    Query Trekton for the CPT and HCPCS codes you bill most often, filtered by the ZIP codes or states where your clients operate. Include the payers you work with so the comparison set is relevant. Save the query so you can refresh it monthly when new MRF data lands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare rates to your book">
    Load your internal allowed amounts into the same view. Trekton shows the market rate distribution (for example, median and spread) for each code, payer, and geography combination. Flag lines where your client's allowed amount sits below the market median or outside the expected range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag outliers and underpayments">
    Filter for cases where the allowed amount is more than one standard deviation below the market mean for that code and payer. These are your highest-confidence underpayment candidates. Prioritize by total dollar impact: a small gap on a high-volume code often matters more than a large gap on a rare code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export a report for negotiation or appeal">
    Export the flagged comparisons into a spreadsheet or PDF. Include the market median, the client's rate, the variance, and the source payer. Use this report during payer contract discussions or as supporting documentation for individual claim appeals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for payer negotiations

Before entering a rate renegotiation, pull the payer's rates across your full code set and compare them to other payers in the same markets. If the payer is consistently below market, you have objective data to support a rate increase request. If the payer is above market, you can use that insight to protect favorable terms.

## Identify facilities and payers with better economics

Trekton lets you compare rates by facility as well as by payer. If a client is considering expanding to a new location or switching a facility contract, you can preview which facilities in that geography have stronger rate profiles with the target payers. This turns facility planning from a guess into a data-driven decision.

<Note>
  Trekton shows negotiated rates, not actual collections. Use the rate data as a leading indicator, then validate with your client's historical remittance data before making contract or operational changes.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Set a monthly reminder to re-run your core query after the latest MRF refresh. Rates shift as payer contracts renew, and early detection of a downward trend gives you more time to respond.
</Tip>
