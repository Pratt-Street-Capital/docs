Walker Health Services manages the entire claims submission lifecycle on your behalf. From the moment encounter data is available in your EMR, our team takes ownership of every step — charge capture, coding compliance, eligibility verification, and electronic submission — so that clean claims reach your payers as quickly as possible.

## Claims Submission Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Charge Capture from EMR">
    Our team pulls encounter data directly from your existing EMR system. No manual data entry or file exports are required on your end. Charges are captured accurately and mapped to the appropriate service codes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coding & Compliance Review">
    Each claim is reviewed for CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS code accuracy. We verify that diagnoses support the services billed and that documentation meets payer-specific billing requirements for behavioral health and residential treatment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility Verification">
    Before submission, we confirm that the patient's insurance coverage is active and that the services rendered are covered under their plan. This step significantly reduces avoidable denials related to eligibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clearinghouse Submission">
    Claims are transmitted electronically through a certified clearinghouse, where they are scrubbed for formatting errors and payer-specific edits prior to reaching the insurance carrier. This adds an additional layer of quality control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payer Acknowledgment & Tracking">
    Once submitted, we monitor each claim for payer acknowledgment and track its status through adjudication. Outstanding or delayed claims are flagged for follow-up before timely filing deadlines are at risk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Claim Types Covered

Walker Health submits both major claim form types used across behavioral health and residential treatment billing:

- **Professional Claims (CMS-1500)** — Used for outpatient services, individual therapy, psychological evaluations, and other provider-rendered services billed under a physician or licensed clinician's NPI.
- **Institutional Claims (UB-04)** — Used for facility-based services including inpatient, residential, and partial hospitalization programs (PHP/IOP) billed under your facility's NPI and revenue codes.

## Timely Filing

Every payer sets its own timely filing deadline — the window in which a claim must be submitted after the date of service. These deadlines typically range from **90 days to 365 days**, depending on the payer and plan type. Walker Health tracks filing deadlines for each payer in your portfolio and prioritizes submission accordingly, so no revenue is forfeited due to missed windows.

<Tip>
  Ensuring patient eligibility is verified before the date of service — rather than at the time of billing — is one of the most effective ways to prevent eligibility-related denials and protect timely filing. Consider building eligibility checks into your intake workflow.
</Tip>

## Turnaround Times

Claims are typically submitted within **1–2 business days** of receiving complete encounter data from your EMR. Submission speed depends on the completeness and accuracy of the documentation provided. Incomplete records or missing authorization numbers may delay submission, so our team will reach out promptly if additional information is needed.
