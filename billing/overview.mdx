## How It Works

The billing lifecycle begins the moment an encounter is documented in your EMR. Walker Health receives that encounter data, reviews it for coding accuracy and payer compliance, and submits a clean claim to the appropriate payer. Once a response is received — whether a payment, adjustment, or denial — our team takes the necessary next steps to ensure your revenue is collected in full.

Every touchpoint in this process is tracked, documented, and reported back to you so you always have a clear picture of your practice's financial performance.

## What We Handle

- **Claims Submission** — Charge entry, coding review, and electronic submission to all major payers
- **Clearinghouse Edits** — Claims are scrubbed for errors before reaching the payer, reducing rejections
- **EOB Reconciliation** — Every explanation of benefits is matched against submitted claims and posted accurately
- **Denial Resolution** — Denied claims are identified, analyzed, and appealed or corrected without delay
- **Appeals Management** — We prepare and submit formal payer appeals with supporting clinical documentation when required
- **Patient Statements** — Outstanding patient balances are billed clearly and in compliance with your practice's policies
