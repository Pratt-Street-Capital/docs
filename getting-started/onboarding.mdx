Getting started with Walker Health Services is designed to be seamless. Our onboarding process is built around your practice's unique needs, ensuring a smooth transition into fully managed revenue cycle operations with minimal disruption to your day-to-day workflows. From your first consultation to go-live, our team is with you every step of the way.

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule a Free Consultation">
    Reach out to the Walker Health Services team to book your complimentary consultation. During this call, we'll discuss your practice's current billing setup, pain points, payer mix, and goals. There's no obligation — just an honest conversation about how we can help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice Needs Assessment">
    Our team conducts a thorough assessment of your practice, reviewing your existing revenue cycle workflows, payer contracts, credentialing status, and EMR setup. This helps us build a tailored service plan that addresses your specific challenges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service Agreement & Plan Setup">
    Once we've aligned on scope and services, we'll finalize a service agreement and configure your account. Your dedicated account manager will walk you through the details and answer any questions before you sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="EMR Integration & Data Migration">
    Our technical team works with you to integrate your Electronic Medical Records (EMR) system with our billing platform. We ensure that patient demographics, insurance information, service records, and clinical data sync accurately and securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff Introduction & Go-Live">
    We introduce your staff to your dedicated Walker Health Services billing and support contacts, confirm all systems are connected and tested, and officially go live. From this point forward, we manage your claims, authorizations, and credentialing on your behalf.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before your onboarding kickoff call, it helps to have the following ready: all **NPI numbers** (individual and organizational), your current **payer contracts and fee schedules**, and your **EMR login credentials** or IT contact information. Having these on hand will speed up the setup process significantly.
</Tip>

## What to Expect in the First 30 Days

The first 30 days after go-live are focused on establishing a stable, accurate billing operation for your practice. Here's what you can expect during that period:

- **Week 1–2:** Your billing team reviews and submits your first batch of claims. Any payer-specific setup quirks or eligibility issues are identified and resolved early.
- **Week 2–3:** You'll receive your first performance snapshot, giving you a baseline view of claim submission volume, acceptance rates, and any outstanding denials.
- **Week 3–4:** Your account manager schedules a 30-day check-in call to review results, address any questions, and fine-tune workflows based on early feedback.

By the end of your first month, you should have a clear picture of your revenue cycle performance and confidence that your billing is in expert hands.
