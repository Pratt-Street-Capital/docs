## How It Works

From your first conversation with our team to ongoing reporting and insights, our process is built to keep your revenue cycle running smoothly. Here's a high-level look at what working with Walker Health Services looks like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule a Consultation">
    Reach out to our team to book a free, no-obligation consultation. We'll learn about your practice, your current billing challenges, and how we can help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding & EMR Setup">
    We conduct a thorough practice needs assessment and integrate with your Electronic Medical Records (EMR) system to establish a seamless data connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We Manage Claims & Billing">
    Our experienced billing team handles claims submission, follow-up, denial management, and payment posting — so you can focus on patient care.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Reports & Insights">
    Access regular performance reports and financial insights that give you full visibility into your revenue cycle and practice health.
  </Step>
</Steps>
