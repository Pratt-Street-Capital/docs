Trekton (also called Radius) is a healthcare price and supply transparency platform that aggregates Machine-Readable Files (MRF) pricing data, commercial payer rates, and facility information across hospitals, psychiatric facilities, rehab centers, and other providers. This page introduces what Trekton does and where to start.

## Who Trekton is for

- **Investors** evaluating healthcare markets, supply trends, and payer rate landscapes
- **Billing companies** benchmarking negotiated rates and allowed amounts across payers
- **Transparency researchers** entering healthcare price transparency and learning how MRF data works
