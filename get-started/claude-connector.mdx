Trekton can answer questions inside Claude (Claude Desktop, claude.ai, and Claude Code) from Trekton's own data. You ask in plain language and Claude looks up the answer in Trekton.

## What you can ask

You can ask four kinds of question.

- **Facilities near a place.** For example, "How many hospitals are within 5 miles of Baltimore?" Claude also opens an interactive panel with a map, a radius slider, facility type chips, a Medicaid tab, and a Demographics tab.
- **One facility.** For example, "Tell me about Mainspring Recovery in Lynchburg, VA." You get the address, phone, ids, beds against peers, listed services, and sources.
- **State Medicaid fee schedules by kind of care.** For example, "What does Virginia Medicaid pay for substance use treatment?" Pick one of four sets: substance use treatment, mental health, hospital, or office visits and common services. You can also ask for specific codes.
- **Area demographics.** For example, "Who lives within 3 miles of there?" You get population, households, income, and the share of people on Medicaid or uninsured. Each new ring is a metered lookup.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an active Trekton account. You sign in with your Microsoft work account when you add the connector, the same sign-in as the app.

## Add it in Claude Desktop or claude.ai

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connector settings">
    Open **Settings**, then **Connectors**, then click **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Name it `Trekton`. Paste the connector URL and the OAuth client ID from your Trekton admin. Leave the client secret blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Sign in with Microsoft if asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    On the Trekton consent screen, check that the address shown is claude.ai, then click **Approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  In Claude Code, run `claude mcp add --transport http --client-id <client id> --callback-port 33418 trekton <connector URL>`, then run `claude mcp login trekton`. The browser opens the same consent screen.
</Note>

## What Claude sees and keeps

Your questions, such as place names, facility names, and codes, travel through the AI app you use. Trekton's data holds no patient information. The connector is read-only and can never change anything in your Trekton account.

## How long it lasts

A connection lasts 30 days. Then Claude asks you to sign in again. A Trekton admin can end every connection at once, and you sign in again on your next question. You can remove the connector from Claude's connector settings at any time.

## Limits

- "Near" is a circle around one point, up to 50 miles. It is not a city or county boundary.
- Medicaid figures are state fee schedules. They are not negotiated prices and not a facility's own prices.
- Answers are kept short: at most 50 facilities or 50 rate lines per question. Ask a narrower question to see others.
- Each account can make 120 requests an hour and read 3,000 rows a day.
- Commercial (insurer) rates are not available yet.

<Tip>
  Ask for a place with its state ("Baltimore, MD") or a ZIP code, and name the kind of care. The panel's slider and chips refine the results without a new question.
</Tip>
