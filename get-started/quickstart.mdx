This guide walks you through requesting access to Trekton, signing in, and finding negotiated rate data. By the end, you will know how to explore the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [https://pratt-street-capital.github.io/radius-demo/](https://pratt-street-capital.github.io/radius-demo/) to request access. The Trekton team will set up your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Credentials">
    After talking to the sales team, your email will be given a green light to log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the dashboard">
    Log in to the Trekton dashboard to browse facilities, payers, and supply insights without writing code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a small state and a single CPT code to keep results manageable while you learn the data shape.
</Tip>
