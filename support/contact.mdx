## Team Contacts

Depending on the nature of your question, the right team can get you to a resolution faster. Use the guide below to direct your inquiry to the appropriate department.

| Topic | Team |
|---|---|
| Billing questions, claim status, payment posting | Revenue Cycle team |
| Prior authorizations, concurrent reviews, appeals | Utilization Review (UR) team |
| Provider enrollment, payer applications, re-credentialing | Credentialing team |
| General inquiries, new client onboarding | Operations team |

For general inquiries and onboarding questions, you can reach our Operations team directly:
