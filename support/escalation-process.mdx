The vast majority of questions and issues are resolved quickly at the account manager level. However, when something is time-sensitive or requires additional authority to address, Walker Health Services has a clear escalation path to make sure your concern reaches the right person without delay.

## Issue Severity Levels

Not every issue requires the same level of urgency. Understanding how Walker Health categorizes issues helps you communicate priority effectively and set accurate expectations for response times.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Urgent" icon="circle-exclamation">
    - Treatment authorization is at risk
    - Immediate billing impact affecting revenue
    - Patient access to care is affected
  </Card>
  <Card title="High" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    - Claim denials totaling over **$5,000**
    - Credentialing deadline within **30 days**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standard" icon="circle-check">
    - Routine billing questions
    - Report requests
    - General process inquiries
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Escalation Path

If your issue is not resolved at the first point of contact, follow these steps to escalate through the appropriate channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Your Account Manager">
    Reach out to your dedicated account manager by phone or email. Include as much relevant detail as possible — patient name, account number, claim number, payer name, and a brief description of the issue — so they can act immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Escalation to Department Director">
    If your issue remains unresolved within **24 hours**, ask your account manager to escalate to the relevant department director. You may also contact the director directly if the matter is urgent. See the director contacts below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the Managing Director">
    For critical issues that require executive attention or cross-departmental coordination, contact the Managing Director directly. This step is reserved for situations where standard escalation has not produced a timely resolution or where the issue carries significant operational or financial risk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response Time Commitments

Walker Health Services is committed to acknowledging and acting on your escalations within the following timeframes:

| Severity | Response Time |
|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Within 2 hours |
| **High** | Within 24 hours |
| **Standard** | Within 2 business days |

Response time begins when your issue is received during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM ET). Issues submitted outside of business hours will be addressed at the start of the next business day.

<Tip>
  When escalating, include the **patient name and account number**, **claim number** (if applicable), **payer name**, and a **brief description of the issue**. The more context you provide upfront, the faster our team can act on your behalf.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Walker Health Services takes a proactive approach to communication. If we identify a billing discrepancy, authorization risk, or credentialing issue affecting your organization, we will notify you before you need to ask. Our goal is to surface and resolve problems early — so escalations are the exception, not the norm.
</Note>
