Medical billing in behavioral health comes with its own set of complexities — from navigating payer-specific requirements to managing denials and patient balances. Whether you are new to working with Walker Health Services or looking to better understand our processes, the answers below address the questions we hear most often from providers like you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does it take to receive payment after a claim is submitted?">
    Payment timelines vary by payer, but most commercial insurance carriers process clean claims within **14 to 30 days** of submission. Medicaid and Medicare timelines differ by state and program, but typically range from **14 to 21 days** for electronic claims. Factors that can extend this timeline include payer backlogs, requests for additional documentation, and claims that require manual review.

    Walker Health monitors all outstanding claims and follows up with payers on your behalf when payments fall outside expected windows. You can track claim statuses through your reporting dashboard or by contacting your billing specialist directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What information do you need from us to begin billing?">
    To onboard your practice and begin submitting claims, Walker Health requires the following:

    - **Practice and facility information:** Legal name, NPI (Type 1 and Type 2), tax ID, and service address
    - **Provider roster:** Individual provider NPIs, credentials, and specialties
    - **Payer contracts and fee schedules:** Copies of all active payer agreements
    - **EMR or practice management system access:** Credentials or a data export method to retrieve patient and charge data
    - **Insurance credentialing status:** Current enrollment status with all payers you accept
    - **Patient demographics and insurance information:** Accurate records for active patients

    Your onboarding specialist will walk you through a checklist during your kickoff call to ensure nothing is missed before billing begins.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are claim denials handled?">
    When a claim is denied, Walker Health reviews the denial reason and determines the appropriate next step. Our denial management process includes:

    1. Identifying the root cause of the denial (e.g., medical necessity, authorization, coding, eligibility)
    2. Gathering any required documentation or corrections
    3. Submitting a corrected claim or formal appeal with supporting documentation
    4. Tracking the appeal through to resolution

    You will be notified of denials that require clinical documentation or information only your team can provide. For all other denials, our team handles the process end to end. You can view denial trends and outcomes in your monthly reporting summary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What billing software or EMR systems do you work with?">
    Walker Health works with a wide range of EMR and practice management platforms commonly used by behavioral health and residential treatment providers, including but not limited to:

    - Kipu
    - Allscripts
    - DrChrono
    - TheraNest
    - SimplePractice
    - CentralReach
    - Netsmart myAvatar
    - Welligent

    If you use a system not listed here, contact your account manager. In most cases, we can accommodate your platform through direct integration, file exports, or a supported data transfer method.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know a claim has been submitted?">
    Once claims are submitted, Walker Health updates claim statuses in your reporting portal. You will be able to see:

    - **Submission date:** When the claim was sent to the payer
    - **Claim status:** Pending, accepted, rejected, denied, or paid
    - **Expected payment date:** Estimated based on payer averages
    - **ERA/EOB details:** Once payment is received

    If you do not have portal access yet or need help interpreting a claim status, reach out to your dedicated billing specialist for a real-time update.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when a payer requests medical records?">
    Payers occasionally request medical records to validate medical necessity before processing a claim. When this happens, Walker Health notifies your clinical team immediately and coordinates the record request. Your team will need to provide the relevant documentation — such as progress notes, treatment plans, or discharge summaries — within the payer's stated deadline.

    <Warning>
      Failing to respond to a medical records request within the payer's deadline can result in a claim denial that is difficult or impossible to overturn. Always respond promptly to documentation requests from our team.
    </Warning>

    Once records are received, Walker Health submits them to the payer and monitors the claim through to payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can you handle both commercial insurance and Medicaid/Medicare billing?">
    Yes. Walker Health handles billing across all payer types, including commercial insurance, managed care organizations (MCOs), Medicaid (including state-specific programs), Medicare, and Medicare Advantage plans. Our team is familiar with the distinct requirements of each payer type and stays current on policy changes that affect behavioral health and substance use treatment services.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are patient balances handled?">
    After your payers have processed a claim and any secondary insurance has been applied, remaining patient balances — such as copays, coinsurance, and deductibles — are calculated and reflected in your accounts receivable. Walker Health can generate patient statements and, depending on your service agreement, manage patient balance follow-up on your behalf.

    If your practice handles patient collections internally, we will provide you with the balance details and documentation needed to bill patients accurately. We recommend a clear financial responsibility policy be communicated to patients at intake to reduce balance disputes downstream.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is your fee structure?">
    Walker Health typically operates on a percentage-of-collections model, meaning our fee is based on the revenue we successfully collect on your behalf. This aligns our incentives with yours — we only earn when you get paid.

    Specific rates vary based on your practice size, payer mix, billing volume, and the services included in your agreement (e.g., billing only, billing plus utilization review, full RCM). Contact our sales team or your account manager for a customized quote based on your organization's needs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a billing discrepancy?">
    If you notice an error in a claim, a payment posting issue, or an unexpected charge on your remittance, contact your Walker Health billing specialist as soon as possible. You can reach out via:

    - **Your client portal:** Submit a support ticket with the claim number and a description of the discrepancy
    - **Email:** Send the details directly to your assigned billing contact
    - **Phone:** Call our billing support line during business hours

    Include the patient name, date of service, claim number (if available), and a description of what appears incorrect. Our team will investigate and respond with findings within two business days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
