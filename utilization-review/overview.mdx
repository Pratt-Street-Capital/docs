Utilization review (UR) is one of the most critical functions in behavioral health revenue cycle management. Payers require clinical justification at every stage of treatment — from admission through discharge — and without a dedicated UR process, your organization risks delayed authorizations, unexpected denials, and significant revenue loss. Walker Health Services manages the full utilization review lifecycle on your behalf, so your clinical team can stay focused on patient care.

## Levels of Care We Support

Walker Health Services provides utilization review across the full continuum of behavioral health and substance use treatment, including:

- **Detox (ASAM 3.7)** — Medically monitored intensive inpatient detoxification
- **Residential Treatment (ASAM 3.5)** — Clinically managed high-intensity residential services
- **Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP / ASAM 2.5)** — Structured daytime programming with medical oversight
- **Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP / ASAM 2.1)** — Multi-day weekly outpatient treatment
- **Outpatient (OP / ASAM 1.0)** — Standard outpatient counseling and medication management

Each level of care carries its own payer-specific criteria, documentation requirements, and authorization timelines. Our team is experienced in navigating the nuances of each.

## Our Utilization Review Team

Your cases are handled by licensed clinical professionals who specialize in behavioral health and substance use treatment. Our UR staff have direct experience working with commercial payers, managed behavioral health organizations (MBHOs), and government programs. They understand payer-specific criteria — including ASAM, Milliman, and InterQual guidelines — and know how to present clinical information in a way that supports approval.

We assign dedicated UR specialists to your account so you always have a point of contact who knows your program, your payer mix, and your documentation workflows.

## How UR Integrates With Billing

<Note>
  Utilization review and medical billing are tightly connected. Every authorization obtained by our UR team is documented and passed directly to your billing workflow, ensuring that claims are submitted with valid auth numbers, correct dates of service, and the appropriate level-of-care codes. This integration significantly reduces the risk of claim denials stemming from missing or expired authorizations, and it creates a clean audit trail if a payer ever questions a paid claim after the fact.
</Note>
